Process and Practice

What I'm Experimenting With

As I'm learning, every photography session is an opportunity to try new things and improve little by little:

AREA // 01

Nighttime & Lights

Exploring the city at night, capturing neon lights, reflections and contrasts. Learning to manage exposure and light sources in low-light conditions.

#Lights #Nighttime #Exposure
AREA // 02

Compositions and Streets

Learning to look for guiding lines, playing with the symmetry of buildings and finding unusual angles in places we usually pass by quickly.

#Composition #Streets #Perspective

Behind the Lens

Starting Out in This World

I've only been into photography for a short while and started simply out of curiosity and fun. I like going for walks with my camera and capturing things that catch my attention, without overcomplicating things with rigid rules.

I have a special weakness for going out at night because the contrast between the darkness and the lights of the city always has a striking touch, but I also enjoy photographing urban scenes during the day, curious corners or landscapes when I visit new places.

My current goal is to keep learning: understand the light better, experiment with editing techniques, find new compositions and, above all, enjoy the process.
